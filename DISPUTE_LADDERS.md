# PROMPT — Dispute Ladders (the Judgment Axis)

The courtroom is secretly about **social behavior under pressure** — "yep, I know that guy,"
not "I get the legal metaphor." **13 behavioral ladders, 6 rungs each (5 · 10 · 20 · 30 · 40 · ★50),
~78 titles.** Each tracks how you act when you're judged, and how you judge others.

## Claude's proofread
- **Clean:** no safety issues, no exact-collisions with locked canon.
- **Two near-twins** with existing names (engine allows both; tweak only for zero confusion):
  - *Comment Section Lawyer* (Disputes Raised 30) ↔ giver combo **Comment Section Legend**
  - *Contrarian* (Contrarianism 30) ↔ giver combo **The Contrarian**
- **Light echoes:** *Consensus Enjoyer* ↔ cross *Consensus Machine* · *Lurking* ↔ receiver combo *The Lurker*.
- **Template density** (meme format — keep or thin, your call): "X Enjoyer" ×5 total · "Receipt" ×4 · "…By Vibes" ×2 apex.
- **Participation** is the soft ladder (your flag) — suggested punch-up noted in that table.
